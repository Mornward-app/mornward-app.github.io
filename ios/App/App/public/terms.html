Terms and privacy · plain words

Mornward collects nothing.

Everything you do in Mornward stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and the app sends nothing about you to anyone.

Who's behind it

Mornward is made by one independent developer based in Thailand. For any question about these terms or about data, email mornward@proton.me.

What stays on your phone

  • The tiny tasks you pick, and any you write yourself.
  • The note you seal for tomorrow morning.
  • Your wake-up time and your evening reminder time.
  • Which mornings you finished, and which science cards you have opened.

This is saved in the app's own storage on your phone. Mornward never sends it anywhere, and it can't: the app makes no network requests.

What Mornward never does

  • Ask you to sign up, or keep an account or a profile.
  • Send your tasks, your note, your times or your progress to a server.
  • Use ads, trackers, analytics tools or third-party code that watches what you do.
  • Read your location, contacts, photos, health data or other apps. It doesn't block or watch any app.
  • Sell, share or use your information for anything, because it never has your information.

Reminders

The morning and evening reminders are notifications your phone schedules for itself. They run on your phone, arrive even when the app is closed, and nothing about them is sent to Mornward. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's Settings.

Earlier beta versions

Until 14 October 2026, beta versions counted a few anonymous numbers about each morning (a random device ID, dates, and yes or no answers, never anything you wrote) and stored them in a database in the United States. Version 1 does not send them. Mornward removes the device ID from your phone when it opens. The records already stored are deleted within 90 days after the beta ends, or sooner if you ask: email us the anonymous ID if you saved it. Without the ID we can't tell which records are yours.

Terms

  • Free. Mornward is provided as it is. Features may change, and things may break.
  • Not medical advice. It's a habit tool, not medical or mental-health advice. If you're struggling, please talk to someone qualified.
  • Your data is yours, and only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your tasks, notes and progress, and they can't be recovered. We have no copy.
  • Age. You're at least 13 years old.
  • Agreeing. Tapping Begin tonight's setup means you accept these terms. If they change, the date below changes.

Your controls

  • Delete everything by deleting the app. That removes all of it from your phone.
  • Turn reminders off in the app, or in Settings, Notifications, Mornward.
  • Questions or complaints. Please email first. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).
