Terms and privacy · plain words

What Mornward records, and what it never does.

Mornward is in free beta. To learn whether it actually helps, it counts a few anonymous things about each morning. It never collects anything you write, and there's no account to sign up for.

Who's behind it

Mornward is made by one independent developer based in Thailand, who decides how this data is used (the "data controller" under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, PDPA). For questions or any request about your data, email mornward@proton.me.

✓ What Mornward keeps track of

  • A random device ID made on your phone, like 3f2a9c…. It isn't linked to your name, email or any account, but it does link your records to each other, so they're pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous. Each record also gets its own random ID, so a record sent twice is stored once.
  • The date, and the time the record arrived.
  • That you opened Mornward, once a day: on iPhone, Android or the web, which screen it opened on, and whether you had already set it up.
  • That you set your morning in the evening: how many tiny tasks you picked (not which), whether one of them is your own, whether you sealed a note (never what it says) and whether the morning reminder is on (never its time).
  • Your morning steps: that you held the sun for all three breaths, or skipped breathing (and after how many), that you ticked off every task, how many tasks that was, your current run of mornings in a row and your total mornings, and that you opened the day's card.
  • Once, when counts start for you: if you used Mornward before counts existed, the dates of the mornings you had already finished, so they aren't lost. Only the dates, nothing else.
  • Whether you opened it as an app (installed, or from your home screen) rather than in a browser tab.

Earlier beta versions (until 8 October 2026) also counted setup answers, morning steps, ratings, which listed app you opened, which first-visit screens you reached, reminder setup taps and which link you came from. Those records are kept and deleted on the same terms.

🔒 What stays private and personal

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account. There's no sign-up.
  • Your tiny tasks, your sealed note, or anything else you write. Not a single word.
  • Your wake-up and evening reminder times, and which tasks you picked.
  • Your location, contacts, photos or other apps. Mornward has no access to them, and doesn't block or watch any app.
  • Anything at all once you turn the counts off.

Beta terms

  • Free beta. Mornward is free while in beta and is provided as it is. Features may change, and things may break.
  • Not medical advice. It's a habit tool, not medical or mental-health advice. If you're struggling, please talk to someone qualified.
  • Your writing lives on your phone. Your tasks, your note and your progress are saved only on your phone (in the app's storage, or this browser's). Deleting the app or clearing your browser data deletes them, and they can't be recovered.
  • Reminders. The morning and evening reminders are notifications your phone schedules itself. Nothing about them is sent to Mornward.
  • Age. You're at least 13 years old.
  • Agreeing. You agree with your first tap on Begin tonight's setup, below a short note that links here. If you set up Mornward before counts existed, you're asked once, and you agree by tapping OK. Nothing is counted before that tap. If these terms change, you're asked again. Agreeing is also your consent to the anonymous counts described here, including storing them in the United States. You can withdraw it any time by turning the counts off.

Your controls

  • Turn the counts off on the first screen, before you tap anything, on the one-time note if you set up before counts existed, or any time later under Privacy and counts on the "See you tonight" screen. Nothing is sent from your phone after that, and anything waiting to be sent is deleted.
  • See or delete your counts. Open Privacy and counts and email the anonymous ID shown there to mornward@proton.me. Every record with that ID is deleted, or sent to you if you asked to see it, within 30 days and usually much sooner. We don't collect your name, email or contact details, so without the ID we can't find your records. Anyone who has your ID could pick out your records, so share it only with us.
  • Delete everything on your phone by deleting the app, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Complain. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, please email first. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Where it goes

The anonymous records are stored in a Supabase database in the United States. The app can only add records; it can't read, change or delete anything, including other people's records. Like most websites, Supabase keeps short-term technical logs of requests, which can include an IP address and browser type. Those aren't used for the beta stats.

The United States may not protect personal data to the same standard as Thai law. The records hold no name, email or contact details, and only the developer can read them. They're used only to learn whether the beta helps, and they're never sold, shared or used for ads.

How long: the records are deleted within 90 days after the beta ends, or sooner if you ask.
