Terms and privacy · plain words

What Mornward records, and what it never does.

Mornward is in free beta. To learn whether it actually helps, it counts a few anonymous things about each morning. It never collects anything you write, and there's no account to sign up for.

Who's behind it

Mornward is made by one independent developer based in Thailand, who decides how this data is used (the "data controller" under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, PDPA). For questions or any request about your data, email mornward@proton.me.

✓ What Mornward keeps track of

  • A random device ID made on your phone, like 3f2a9c…. It isn't linked to your name, email or any account.
  • The date, and the time the record arrived.
  • Your setup answers: what you usually open in bed (social, games, videos, news or other), which apps you picked from the list, and how many of your own you added.
  • How your morning went: minutes, streak, breathing done or skipped, how many get-up boxes you ticked, how often you asked for different ones, and whether you wrote an intention or had a note waiting.
  • Your rating (👎 / 👌 / 👍), if you tap one.
  • Which app you opened from the one-job list, if any. Apps you added yourself are just counted as "own".
  • Whether you opened Mornward from your home screen.

🔒 What stays private and personal

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account. There's no sign-up.
  • Your notes to tomorrow-you. Not a single word.
  • Your intention. Only whether you wrote one.
  • Your own get-up steps, or which get-up options you saw or ticked. Only how many.
  • The names or web addresses of apps you added yourself.
  • What you search for on YouTube, Reddit, TikTok or anywhere else.
  • Which quote or opening line you saw.
  • Your location, contacts, photos or other apps. Mornward has no access to them.
  • Anything at all once you tap Turn off.

Beta terms

  • Free beta. Mornward is free while in beta and is provided as it is. Features may change, and things may break.
  • Not medical advice. It's a habit tool, not medical or mental-health advice. If you're struggling, please talk to someone qualified.
  • Your writing lives on your phone. Notes, intentions and your own steps are saved only in your browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them, and they can't be recovered.
  • Age. You're at least 13 years old.
  • Agreeing. You agree once, the first time you open Mornward. If these terms change, you'll be asked again. Agreeing is also your consent to the anonymous stats described here, including storing them in the United States. You can withdraw it any time by turning stats off.

Your controls

  • Turn stats off at the bottom of the last screen of any morning. Nothing is sent from your phone after that, and anything waiting to be sent is deleted.
  • See or delete your stats. At the end of any morning, open Show my ID / delete my data and email the ID shown there to mornward@proton.me. Every record with that ID is deleted, or sent to you if you asked to see it, within 30 days and usually much sooner. Without the ID there's no way to tell which records are yours, because nothing else identifies you.
  • Delete everything on your phone with the button in the same place. Everything you write stays on your phone, in this browser's storage, so clearing your browser data deletes it too.
  • Complain. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, please email first. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Where it goes

The anonymous records are stored in a Supabase database in the United States. The app can only add records; it can't read, change or delete anything, including other people's records. Like most websites, Supabase keeps short-term technical logs of requests, which can include an IP address and browser type. Those aren't used for the beta stats.

The United States may not protect personal data to the same standard as Thai law. The records hold no name, email or contact details, and only the developer can read them. They're used only to learn whether the beta helps, and they're never sold, shared or used for ads.

How long: the records are deleted within 90 days after the beta ends, or sooner if you ask.
